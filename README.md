# Hong Kong Speedrun 香港一日

A ~10-minute browser game: six mini-rounds across one chaotic Hong Kong day.

- 07:48 MTR Seat Wars — beat the aunties to empty seats
- 08:25 Cha Chaan Teng — decode the waiter's slang (靚仔 is rice, not a handsome boy)
- 12:30 Yum Cha Rush — grab the right dim sum off the carts
- 18:10 Mong Kok Drip — dodge air-con drips, catch egg waffles
- 21:40 Minibus 有落! — shout your stop loud enough, early enough
- 23:15 Nano Flat — Tetris your life into 220 sq ft

Single static `index.html`, no build step. Keyboard or touch.
